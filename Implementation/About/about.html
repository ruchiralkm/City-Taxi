Eco-friendly

Sustainability

City-Taxi is committed to becoming a fully electric, zero-emission platform by 2040, with all rides in zero-emission vehicles, on public transit, or using micromobility. As a leading mobility platform, we aim to address climate change by offering more green ride options, supporting drivers in going electric, prioritizing transparency, and partnering with NGOs and private sectors for a clean energy transition.
